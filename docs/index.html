규칙

  • 4×4 판에 X가 먼저, 한 칸씩 번갈아 둡니다.
  • 자기 말 3개가 가로·세로·대각선으로 한 줄이 되면 그 사람이 집니다.
  • 판이 다 찰 때까지 아무도 줄을 만들지 않으면 무승부입니다.

개인정보처리방침

Reverse Tic-Tac-Toe

Whoever makes a line first loses

Rules

  • On a 4×4 board, X goes first; players take turns placing one mark.
  • If three of your own marks form a line — across, down or diagonal — you lose.
  • If the board fills up without a line, it's a draw.

FAQ

How do I reset my record?
Your record is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

What are the red dots?
With "Show danger" on, a red dot marks every cell that would lose right now. You can turn it off below the board.
